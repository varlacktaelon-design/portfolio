Nikola Jokic 2023 Finals Statistics (vs. Heat)

Jokic PPG

Nikola Jokic averaged a dominant 30.2 points per game in the 2023 Finals against the Miami Heat. He demonstrated his complete offensive arsenal, hitting critical shots and maintaining high efficiency to lead the Nuggets to their first championship and earn the Finals MVP award. His unguardable interior game forced the Heat to constantly send double teams, which he expertly dissected with precise passes. Jokic became the first player in NBA history to lead all players in the playoffs in total points, rebounds, and assists.

Jokic APG

Jokic's elite playmaking was evident with 7.2 assists per game in the Finals. His ability to read the defense and deliver precise passes from the elbow or post was the engine of the Nuggets' offense, constantly setting up teammates for easy baskets. He utilized his size and superior court vision to consistently make skip passes and hit cutters from anywhere on the floor. This selfless distribution prevented the opposing team from ever successfully containing Denver's deep offensive attack.

Jokic RPG

Jokic commanded the paint, pulling down 14.0 rebounds per game, ensuring defensive stops ended possessions and securing offensive boards for second-chance opportunities. This made him a threat to record a triple-double in every Finals contest. His positional awareness and strong boxing out prevented the smaller Miami Heat team from generating critical extra chances. By controlling the glass, he ensured the Nuggets dictated the pace and limited the transition opportunities for their opponents.
