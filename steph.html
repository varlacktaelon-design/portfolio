Stephen Curry 2022 Finals Statistics (vs. Celtics)

Curry PPG

Stephen Curry was sensational in the 2022 Finals, averaging 31.2 points per game against the Boston Celtics. His unparalleled shooting and ability to create offense single-handedly powered the Warriors to their fourth championship in eight years, earning him his first career Finals MVP. He achieved this while maintaining high efficiency, particularly with an incredible performance in Game 4 where his 43 points single-handedly saved the series. His continuous motion and off-ball threat stretched Boston's defense to its breaking point, opening up the floor for the rest of the Warriors' offense.

Curry Game 4
Curry APG

Curry demonstrated his playmaking chops with 5.0 assists per game in the Finals. His gravity opened up the court for teammates, and he consistently found cutters and shooters, proving he's much more than just a scorer for the Warriors' complex offense. Defenders had to commit so heavily to preventing his shots that it routinely led to uncontested layups and open three-pointers for his supporting cast. This constant threat forced the Celtics to abandon their defensive rotations, allowing the Warriors' movement to flourish.

Curry RPG

Stephen Curry grabbed 6.0 rebounds per game in the 2022 Finals, showcasing his commitment on both ends of the floor. His surprising effectiveness on the boards helped the Warriors control possessions and push the pace, vital for their championship success. He was relentless in tracking down long rebounds created by his team's defensive pressure, often initiating the fast break immediately. This collective effort on the glass neutralized the Celtics' size advantage and reinforced Curry's role as a true all-around floor leader.
