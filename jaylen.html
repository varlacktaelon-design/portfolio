Jaylen Brown Finals Statistics

JB PPG

Jaylen Brown averaged 20.8 points per game during the Finals, contributing to the team's championship run. His consistent offensive pressure kept the defense honest, opening up opportunities for teammates. He was highly efficient, demonstrating mastery in both driving to the basket and hitting key jumpers. This multifaceted attack ultimately earned him the Bill Russell Finals MVP Award. His performance solidified his status as a complete two-way superstar for the team.

JB APG

Brown dished out 5.0 assists per game in the Finals. His role expanded beyond scoring, showcasing improved vision as a primary ball-handler. This playmaking was crucial in countering defensive traps and finding open shooters, making the team's attack more dynamic and unpredictable. He consistently made the right reads out of double-teams, keeping the offense flowing smoothly when the pressure was highest. This enhanced facilitation helped turn close games into comfortable wins throughout the series.

JB RPG

Jaylen Brown grabbed 5.4 rebounds per game in the Finals. His effort on the glass, especially securing defensive rebounds, helped the team limit second-chance points and quickly launch fast breaks. This highlighted his all-around commitment to winning beyond just his scoring duties. He consistently attacked the paint to secure crucial defensive boards against bigger opponents. This tenacity on the glass was a quiet but significant factor in the team's ability to maintain pace and control the tempo of the series.
